Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What We Collect

When you subscribe to Onside+, your payment information (card details, billing address) is collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe — Onside Data never receives or stores your full card details. Stripe collects the email address and billing information you provide at checkout to manage your subscription.

2. What We Don't Collect

This site does not currently use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers. We do not collect browsing behavior, device fingerprints, or location data beyond what your browser sends as part of normal web requests.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — processes payments and manages subscription billing. See Stripe's own privacy policy for details on how they handle your payment data.
  • football-data.org — supplies match, fixture, and statistics data. This is a one-way data pull for content purposes; no personal user information is shared with this provider.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Stripe is used solely to manage your subscription — processing payments, sending billing-related communications, and identifying your account if you contact us about your subscription.

5. Data Retention

Subscription and billing records are retained by Stripe in accordance with their own data retention practices and applicable law. We do not maintain a separate database of subscriber personal information beyond what's necessary to operate the site.

6. Your Rights

You can view, update, or cancel your subscription at any time through the subscription management portal linked in the site footer. If you have questions about your data or wish to request its deletion, you can reach out through the contact details provided on this site.

7. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 18 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed through our subscription management portal or the contact details provided on this site.
